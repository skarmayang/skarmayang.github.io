A little about me

A computer science girl from the land of high passes

I grew up in Leh and went to school there, then left the mountains for a B.Tech in Bareilly and an M.Tech in computer science at IIT Bombay. These days I work on compilers in Pune — the software that turns the code people write into something a processor can actually run.

The parts of computing I keep coming back to are program analysis. The rest of my time goes to travelling, filming it, and putting it here. I'm from the highest mountains in the country and I still can't say no to a beach.
